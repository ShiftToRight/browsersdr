# Independent APRS modem-audio fixture

The unchanged source WAV is 639436 bytes, SHA-256 `4b3119170d9e5981532ce67cd67f21f3ef99064d7f9db7a691800a1a0d9b1375`. Its mono, 44.1 ksample/s, signed 16-bit little-endian data is complete enough for three frames, but its unfinalized header declares more frames than the bytes present. Extraction uses actual available PCM bytes, not the declared duration. `picoSoundcard.s16le` contains unchanged PCM samples **17640 through 74969 inclusive** (1.3 seconds, 114660 bytes), extracted from the first transmission without filtering, resampling or amplitude adjustment. Excerpt SHA-256 `b71eb320569c937bb3891e1de49f2dc34c52c324f044122671606a9aca07b17b`.

Expected station, destination, path and coordinates come from the independently authored `src/aprs_pico_beacon_demo.c` at the same revision (SHA-256 `57a67c105b239c8e3953c082ff4a6e4cc4329536277748cf10993b697da71573`): DL3TG-9 → APPIPI, WIDE1-1/WIDE2-2, 48.75588°/9.19011°, 483 m, comment linking to that project. The encoded altitude is 1585 feet; rounding permits less than 0.5 m difference from the configured value. Exact information bytes and extraction metadata are retained in `picoSoundcard.json`.

`../independentAudio.test.ts` checks the excerpt digest, real Bell-202/HDLC/FCS/AX.25/APRS decoding, expected field values, bounded sample ranges and three block partitions. It never invokes BrowserSDR's synthetic signal encoder. Starting the demodulator at the excerpt boundary shifts measured timing by at most one sample relative to the complete upstream WAV. This verifies the audio/framing/parser path, not the RF front-end, channel selection or hardware acceptance.
